# ReLoop_Waste_Managament_System
ReLoop helps people give everyday items a second life. Use Scrap Vision to estimate scrap value, Book Loop to buy or sell used books, and Sound Check to get a basic appliance condition suggestion. Track your reuse activity and ReLoop points in one place. Try it at reloopai.streamlit.app.

ReLoop 

ReLoop helps you keep useful things in circulation. Estimate scrap value, pass books on to other readers, check an appliance's sound, and track your contributions in one place.

🌐 **Open ReLoop:** [https://reloopai.streamlit.app/](https://reloopai.streamlit.app/)

## Get started

1. Open ReLoop and create an account, or sign in.
2. Choose **Scrap Vision**, **Book Loop**, **Sound Check**, or **Impact** from the navigation bar.

## Scrap Vision

Upload photos of your scrap or use your camera. Review the detected materials and estimated weights, and edit the table if needed. ReLoop shows an estimated value and local dealer offers for your selected area. Use the map icon for a shop's location or the contact button to arrange pickup.

## Book Loop

Browse books listed by other users and filter by category, subject, or title. Select **Request this book** to contact its owner. To sell a book, select **List a book**, enter its details and price, then publish it. Your listings and incoming requests appear in **Your books for sale**. Mark a listing **Sold** when it has sold; this removes it from active listings and awards 100 ReLoop points.

## Sound Check

Select the appliance type, then record a short sound or upload a WAV or MP3 file. ReLoop provides a suggested Resell, Repair, or Scrap verdict based on audio signals. Treat this as a basic guide, not a professional inspection.

## Impact

See your saved scrap valuations, book activity, appliance checks, and total value unlocked. ReLoop points are earned by marking listed books Sold: each sale adds 100 points. The level indicator shows your current level and progress toward the next one.

## Your account

Open **Account** to view or update your profile details and sign out. Use a valid email and phone number so other users can contact you about book requests.

> **Please note:** Scrap weights, prices, and dealer offers are estimates and can vary. Confirm the item, price, and pickup details directly with the buyer before completing a sale.
